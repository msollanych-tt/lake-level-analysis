Coquitlam Lake summer drawdown

Conclusions

    Detected flow events

    From lake level, 08MH002, 08MH141, and WUP fish-flow targets. Uncheck Summer Focus to see spring shading on the charts. News can confirm a storm or a work notice; this pipeline does not scrape it.

      How to read this

      • Hover for exact values; click legend items to isolate a year.
      • dH/dt is lake-level change over a seven-day window. Negative means the lake is falling. dQ/dt is the river-discharge change at Port Coquitlam.
      • Volumes and “river as lake-cm/day” assume a 12.5 km² surface. That is a first-order conversion, not a stage-storage curve. 08MH002 includes tributaries, so the river-as-cm/day line is an upper bound on dam release.
      • WUP fish flows from Coquitlam Dam are 1–6 m³/s by month (Treatment 2 order). A 50–100 m³/s pulse is not a mandatory fish release. The dotted grey line on the river chart is that dam-release target, not a 08MH002 forecast.
      • Freshet vs spill: Coquitlam is rain-dominated. Fraser snowmelt freshet is typically May–June. If 08MH141 spikes and the lake rises, that is inflow. If 08MH002 stays high after 08MH141 collapses and the lake falls, that is controlled spill / flood storage. A construction drawdown would look like the second pattern with no preceding inflow spike — hydrometrics cannot read the work order.
      • Dual-axis charts: blue is the left axis, orange is the right. On the change-rate comparison, both axes are cm/day with the same numeric range, so zero is one shared line.

      Lake level by calendar date

      Lines are individual years aligned by month and day; the shaded band is the historical 10th–90th percentile range. Unit: metres, WSC assumed datum.

      Lake level-change rate

      Seven-day mean dH/dt. Negative values mean the lake is falling. Unit: centimetres per day.

      Cumulative lake drawdown

      Fall in lake level since the first observation in the visible period. 1 cm ≈ 0.125 million m³ at 12.5 km². Unit: centimetres.

      Downstream river discharge

      Orange: 08MH002 at Port Coquitlam (dam release plus lower tributaries). Teal: 08MH141 above the lake (partial inflow). Dotted grey: WUP dam fish-flow target (1–6 m³/s). Unit: m³/s.

      River flow-change rate

      Seven-day mean dQ/dt at 08MH002. Positive values mean downstream river flow is rising. Unit: m³/s per day.

      Lake level and river discharge

      Left / blue: lake level in metres. Right / orange: river discharge in m³/s. Compare timing, not magnitude. Lake zero is not shown; forcing it would flatten the seasonal signal.

      Lake drop vs river-equivalent drop

      Left / blue: actual lake dH/dt (cm/day). Right / orange: 08MH002 converted to lake-cm/day using 12.5 km². Same numeric range, so zero is one line. If orange sits well below the blue drop, river release cannot explain the drawdown.
